PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS
	EN	NOAA Weather Alerts (USA)

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_DESC
	EN	Plays U.S. National Weather Service alerts on your Squeezeboxes, then restores what was playing. Unofficial, and not affiliated with NOAA.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_STATUS
	EN	Alert feed

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_FEED_OK
	EN	Last checked:

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_ACTIVE
	EN	Active alerts for your zones:

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_FEED_BAD
	EN	Alerts are not being received.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_BACKUP
	EN	This plugin needs power, your network and the internet. Keep a battery-backed weather radio as your primary warning.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_TEST
	EN	Test

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_TEST_DESC
	EN	Plays a test alert on every selected player at the test volume, then restores them.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_SEND_TEST
	EN	Send test alert

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_STOP
	EN	Stop alert

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_ZONES
	EN	Alert zones

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_ZONES_DESC
	EN	Your National Weather Service county and forecast zone codes, separated by commas, such as INC043,INZ091.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_POLL
	EN	Check every (seconds)

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_POLL_DESC
	EN	How often to check for new alerts, from 30 to 3600 seconds.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_EVENTS
	EN	Alert types

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_EVENTS_DESC
	EN	Choose what each alert type does. Warnings set to interrupt every player ignore quiet hours.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_LEVEL_INTERRUPT
	EN	Interrupt every player

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_LEVEL_ANNOUNCE
	EN	Interrupt players already on

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_LEVEL_DISPLAY
	EN	Show on screen only

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_LEVEL_OFF
	EN	Off

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_VOLUME
	EN	Alert volume

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_VOLUME_DESC
	EN	Volume for real alerts, from 1 to 100.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_TEST_VOLUME
	EN	Test volume

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_TEST_VOLUME_DESC
	EN	Volume for test alerts, from 1 to 100.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_PLAYERS
	EN	Players

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_PLAYERS_DESC
	EN	Tick the players that receive alerts. Enter a volume to override the alert volume for that player.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_QUIET
	EN	Quiet hours

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_QUIET_DESC
	EN	Holds lower-priority alerts until the end of this window. Use 24-hour times.

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_QUIET_ON
	EN	Use quiet hours

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_PIPER
	EN	Piper speech server

PLUGIN_WEATHERALERTS_PIPER_DESC
	EN	Optional. The URL of a Piper HTTP server, used to read the full alert text. Without one, a short recorded phrase is used.
